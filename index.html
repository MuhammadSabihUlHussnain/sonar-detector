mAP50 is the usual detection score (0 to 1): how well boxes are found and placed, counting a box as right when it overlaps the true one by at least half. Above about 0.6 is good; under 0.3 means the model often misses or mislabels that class.

Reading the result
  • Confidence is the model's own certainty for a box. Lower the slider to see weaker guesses, raise it to keep only sure ones.
  • A detection is only as trustworthy as its class's test accuracy. Check the right-hand column of the detections table.
  • The model knows 25 object classes from sonar datasets. Anything outside them (rocks, seabed texture, other wrecks) may be missed or given the nearest class.
  • Seeing nothing can mean there is nothing, or that the image is unlike the training data (a different sonar, range or colour map).

Model: YOLO26s (Ultralytics) trained on a mixed sonar dataset: UATD, Nankai FLS, Marine Debris FLS, Caltech Fish Counting, AI4Shipwrecks, SCTD, MILCO/NOMBO, AquaScan-1K and GhostVision. See the README for sources and licenses.
